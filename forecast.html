    Words used here

    Plain meanings of the trading terms on this page. Click to open.

    Reference
    Buy (long)
    You own shares and make money if the price goes up.
    Short
    You borrow shares and sell them, then buy them back later. You make money if the price goes down. Needs a margin account.
    Round-trip cost
    What buying and then selling costs you in total: fees plus the spread. 0.05% of $25 is about 1 cent.
    Spread
    The gap between the price buyers pay and sellers get. "Commission-free" apps still charge you this.
    bps (basis points)
    Hundredths of a percent. 5 bps = 0.05%.
    Win chance
    How often a trade like this ends with more money than it started, after costs.
    Expected profit
    The average result if you made this same trade many times. Any single trade can win or lose much more.
    Bad case (1 in 20)
    A loss this big or bigger happens about once every 20 trades.
    Gap
    How far the opening price is from yesterday's close.
    Top fifth
    The best-ranked 20% of the ~500 S&P 500 stocks on a signal that day.
    Tested / passed
    Checked on 2010–2018, again on 2019–2022, and once more on 2023–today data that stayed locked until the end.
    t (t-stat)
    How sure a result is not luck. Above about 2 is unlikely to be luck. Above 3 is strong.
    Margin account
    A brokerage account that lets you borrow. Needed for shorting.
