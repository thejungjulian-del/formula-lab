Positions

Every stock the simulation (or you) bought. Unrealized = gain or loss on shares still held, at the latest close. Realized = profit or loss locked in when it was sold. Sold stocks are greyed out.

Forecast accuracy and learning

Once a forecast's check date passes, its prediction is compared with what the stock actually did. Every result counts, right or wrong. The scale k is the adjustment applied to new forecasts: below 1 means your forecasts have been too big, above 1 too small. It starts at 1 and moves slowly, because a handful of results is mostly luck.
